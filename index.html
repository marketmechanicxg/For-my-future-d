Hey babe,

So I've been sitting here thinking about everything that's been going on between us. And I just wanna say I'm sorry. Not the fake kinda sorry, but the one that's coming straight from my heart.

I know I've been pissing you off a lot lately. Whether it's me being clueless, too distant, or just my attitude wearing you down. I know you've probably been crazy patient with me, and I really regret it if your patience is starting to run out. I don't wanna lose you because of my own stupidity.

I've been thinking a lot, and I realized one thing I don't wanna keep being the guy who stresses you out. I wanna change. Not just sweet promises, but I actually wanna be a better version of myself for you, for us, and for the future we wanna build together.

I want us to have a beautiful future. I want us to laugh together without all this built-up frustration. I want us to have each other's backs, understand each other, and grow together. And none of that's gonna happen if I stay the same person I am right now. So I'm asking you for a chance to prove I'm serious.

I'm not asking you to trust me right away. I'm just asking for time and space to show you I really wanna change. I'll learn to be more attentive, more patient, and more understanding. If I mess up again, call me out. If I forget, remind me. I don't wanna lose us just because of my ego or bad habits I never bothered to fix.

You mean so much to me. More than I can put into words. I don't wanna lose your smile, your laugh, or even the way you get mad at me when I screw up. All of that matters because it's part of you. And I wanna keep being by your side not as someone who drains you, but as someone who makes you feel safe and loved.

I'm sorry, babe. Sorry I haven't been the best for you. But I promise, I'll keep trying to be better. For us. For that beautiful future we wanna build together.
